# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small business owners in India — bakeries, dental clinics, restaurants, tailors, laundromats. Mostly non-technical, primarily on mobile. They're busy running their business and need tools that work without a learning curve. Their customers scan a QR code at the counter to leave a review.

## Product Purpose

Revify removes the "blank page" problem from Google and Yelp reviews. A business owner signs up, creates a profile, and gets a custom review page linked to a QR code. Their customers scan it, pick a star rating, get an AI-generated review they can edit, and post directly — all in under 20 seconds. The landing page is the product entry point, not a separate marketing surface. Success looks like more reviews for the business with zero friction for the customer.

## Brand Personality

Warm and approachable. Friendly without being cutesy. The tool should feel like a helpful partner, not enterprise software. Think Notion or Canva — clean, inviting, respects the user's intelligence without overwhelming them. Confident but never cold.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise SaaS (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho) — heavy, intimidating, too many options on screen
- Template-driven sites (Bootstrap defaults, WordPress admin) — generic, cluttered, no personality
- Avoid the "dashboard sprawl" pattern: dense data tables, sidebars with 20 nav items, every metric shown at once

## Design Principles

- **Mobile-first, always.** Most users and their customers are on phones. Design for thumbs, not cursors. Every interaction must work on a small screen before scaling up.
- **Simplicity is the product.** Users are not tech-savvy. Every extra step, field, or option is a reason to leave. Ruthlessly cut what doesn't serve the core flow.
- **Warm over corporate.** Rounded shapes, friendly copy, welcoming contrast. The interface should feel like a conversation with a helpful friend, not a form at a government office.
- **Show, don't explain.** The product demonstrates its own value. The landing page mockup shows the review flow. The dashboard shows results. No onboarding wizards, no tutorial overlays.
- **Earn every pixel.** No decoration for decoration's sake. No feature without a user need. The best interface is the one the user doesn't notice because it just works.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. Good contrast ratios, especially on mobile. Welcoming, readable text for users who may have varying levels of visual acuity. Semantic HTML for screen readers. Support reduced-motion preferences. Consider that many users may be in bright outdoor environments (street-facing shops) — contrast must hold in direct sunlight.
