How it works

From signup to your first review in under 3 minutes

No technical knowledge needed. If you can print a piece of paper, you can use Revify.

01
🏪

Create your business profile

Sign up, tell us about your business — what you do, what makes you special. We pull in your existing reviews and build a summary automatically.

02
📲

Print your unique QR code

We generate a QR code linked to your Revify review page. Print it, stick it on your counter, receipt, or table — wherever your customers are.

03
✨

Customers tap stars, AI does the rest

Your customer scans, picks a star rating, and gets a beautifully written review in seconds. They can pick a tone, tweak it, and post to Google — done.

Features

Everything a small business actually needs

No bloat. No enterprise complexity. Just the things that get you more reviews.

⭐

Stars → Instant AI Review

Customer picks 1–5 stars and gets a fully written, context-aware review based on your business details. No blank box, no writer's block.

AI-powered
🎭

Tone selector

Customers who want more control can pick a tone — Friendly, Formal, Detailed, Enthusiastic — or write a short note and let AI rewrite it in their chosen style.

Personalisation
📊

Review dashboard

See every review that came through Revify, your average rating over time, and which tone customers are choosing most. Built-in, no integrations needed.

🔗

Direct Google posting

One tap redirects customers to your Google Business review page with the AI text pre-filled in their clipboard. Friction: near zero.

Business intelligence snapshot

We pull together your existing reviews across Google into a plain-English summary — your strengths, common praises, and areas customers mention most. Your customers see this when they scan, so they know what to expect.
